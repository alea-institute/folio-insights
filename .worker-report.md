The plan explicitly identifies its Phase 9/11/13 deltas and retires active `elaborates` without inventing relations for old history.

- **Anchored scores (R5):** Damien has not verified per-genre anchors. Every score remains “judge agreement, unanchored” until his authored verification exists.
- **All public brief cells:** no 168-document corpus was fetched or rights-cleared during planning. A publicly filed private/state-authored brief is not automatically a public-domain federal work or government edict. Every acquired item needs its actual license/public-domain/permission basis; missing rights leaves that cell incomplete. The [Copyright Office guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/chap300/ch300-copyrightable-authorship.pdf) and [USAGov government-copyright guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright) support that distinction.

Those are observed fetch failures, not proof that the jurisdiction is unavailable. Network acquisition units must use permitted official alternatives or record the exact gap.
- **Quality targets:** no measured claim of P ≥ 0.95 / R ≥ 0.90 was made. U9/U32 establish achieved baselines; final success remains contingent on real held-out results.
