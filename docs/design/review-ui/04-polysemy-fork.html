What connects these senses?

Term under review: consideration

Sense A · proposed prime

Contract consideration

A bargained-for exchange supporting a promise.

The promised service supplies the consideration for the return promise.
Sense B · related use

Taking into consideration

Giving a fact attention while forming a judgment.

Take the witness’s vantage point into consideration before weighing the account.

The focal sense for this proposed analogy. fi:primeAnalogate and fi:analogousTo will reference the same selected sense.
